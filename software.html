Simulation models

One hand description, exported to the three formats most simulators read. The wrist frame and the three fingertip sensor frames are defined as in the paper.

FormatUse it withContents
URDFROS, PyBullet, Isaac Gym, general toolingLinks, joints, limits, inertias, visual and simplified collision meshes
MJCFMuJoCoAs above, plus position actuators and fingertip force sites
USDIsaac Sim, Isaac LabArticulation with drives and collision approximations

What the models are for. They describe the hand: kinematics, workspace, grasping, policy prototyping. They do not model the removal tasks. Hot-melt bonds, Blu-Tack and granular media are hard to simulate faithfully, so results on the task suite are reported on the real robot.

Learning setup in the paper

An Action Chunking Transformer fuses vision, joint state, fingertip pose and wrist-frame force, and predicts joint-displacement chunks. There is no state machine, contact threshold or hand-written force controller.
